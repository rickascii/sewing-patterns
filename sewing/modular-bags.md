# Modules
## Individual modules
	- A small bag for daily essentials (phone, wallet, keys)
		- Should include some nice way of keeping keys in particular separate
		- Should also include a subpocket for a pocketknife or anything else not allowed on a plane
	- A larger bag for large knitting projects
		- A very long scarf, or a sweater, or maybe if I decide to go for leg warmers.
	- A medium-sized bag for small knitting projects
		- Shouldn't have more than one _maybe_ two of these at a time
		- Small means something like a hat or a cowl. In principle could be done with one ball of yarn.
    - A medium/large bag for the gym
        - Should certainly have a segregated section for dirty clothes.
        - Might do a waterproof thing there, or lanolized wool. Or hell, lanolized cotton might do.
## Academic backpack
    - A medium-sized bag for notebooks and pens
        - I already have the pen case, so we just need a sleeve or something for that
        - I'll probably need to settle on a notebook size. I might bump up from A5 to A4.
	- A small-ish bag for board supplies
		- A shock-insulated (polyfill?) section for chalk
		- A section for black markers
		- A section for colored markers
		- A section for a chalkboard eraser
		- Whiteboard eraser is probably superfluous for the time being, since UIC uses the rags
    - A compartment for my laptop
        - This might determine the size of the backpack
        - Should be shock-insulated and ideally water-insulated as well
        - Granted, if I can water-insulate the whole backoack with the off-brand goretex then that should do as well
## Saddle bags
    - Should come in a few sizes - small medium large should do
        - Large should carry my instant pot and beer boxes - not simultaneously, but whichever is bigger
        - Medium should work for most single-person grocery runs
        - The main thing here is that they should be relatively easy to attach and detach, but also lock on.
        - The large ones in particular might get an aluminum frame if I can swing it.
            - It's very unclear if I could swing that.
